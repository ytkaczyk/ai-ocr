# Small Amount of Sugar:

Not only is kombucha a sparkling and pleasant-tasting beverage, but it is also an excellent alternative to sugary drinks such as sodas and industrial juices because it contains almost no sugar. You might be thinking that to prepare kombucha, we need sweetened tea, so how is it that this drink contains only a small amount of sugar? It's thanks to fermentation. During this process, bacteria and yeasts convert sugar into various compounds, including organic acids and a little alcohol (ethanol). The longer the fermentation period, the more likely the sugar is to be consumed, which is why it is a healthy, low-sugar beverage.
