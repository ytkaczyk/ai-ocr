# Can you lose weight with kombucha?

If you think that drinking kombucha and gorging on pizza, burgers, and sweets at the same time is allowed and can help you lose weight just because you are drinking kombucha, then the answer is no! The key to successfully losing weight is to follow a balanced diet while regularly engaging in physical exercise (if you are too lazy to do sports daily, at least 30 minutes of walking per day is effective to help you maintain good physical shape).

So, kombucha alone is not an option for losing weight, but if it is incorporated into a balanced diet, it can obviously help you achieve your weight loss goal.

This healthy drink is generally low in calories compared to other beverages. To lose weight, there needs to be a balance between calorie intake and energy expenditure. In conclusion, a balanced diet, managing the portions you eat, physical exercise, and kombucha can help you achieve significant results in terms of weight loss. Therefore, kombucha is part of a healthy lifestyle and indirectly contributes to weight loss.

## How much kombucha should you drink each day?

Scientifically speaking, there is no evidence yet on the adequate amount of kombucha to drink per day. This amount can vary from person to person depending on several factors, including your individual tolerance, personal preferences, and health goals. It is best to start with small amounts if you have never consumed kombucha before, to see how your body reacts to this sparkling drink and also to allow your intestines to adapt to the new microorganisms contained in kombucha. It is wise to start with half a glass to one glass per day. You can then gradually increase this dose as you get used to it; some people may consume up to 1 liter per day.

It should be noted that kombucha contains a small amount of alcohol. In this case, people sensitive to alcohol should be very careful about their amount of kombucha consumption.
